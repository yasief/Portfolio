Learning velocity over credentials.

// The questions I get asked most

Frequently asked

Short, straight answers. For anything not covered here, the Contact section is the fastest route.

What exactly do you do?
I'm an IT Administrator & ERP Specialist based in Dubai. In practice I keep business systems running end-to-end — ERP platforms (Odoo, Zoho), cloud and networking (AWS, IoT device fleets), and cybersecurity — with a track record of 99.7% uptime across smart-tech and multi-brand environments.
Which ERP platforms do you specialise in?
Primarily Odoo and Zoho — configuration, module setup, workflow automation, third-party integrations and ongoing administration. My focus is making ERP actually reduce manual work rather than add to it.
What's your cloud & infrastructure background?
AWS, network administration, and IoT device fleets across hybrid on-prem/cloud setups — plus the monitoring and uptime engineering that keeps them reliable. Network topology, provisioning and incident response are day-to-day work for me.
How do you approach cybersecurity?
Defensive-first and practical: least-privilege access, timely patching, tested backups and disaster recovery, monitoring, and user awareness. The goal is resilience — systems that keep running and recover fast when something goes wrong.
What results have you delivered?
99.7% uptime across the environments I run, meaningful reductions in manual effort through ERP automation, and consolidated IT operations across multiple brands under one smart-tech umbrella.
What languages do you speak?
English, Tamil, Malayalam and Arabic — useful across Dubai's multinational teams and vendor relationships.
What makes you different from other IT admins?
I pair hands-on systems administration with real ERP depth and marketing/analytics literacy — so I connect IT to business outcomes, not just keep the lights on. I'm as comfortable in a server console as I am mapping a workflow to what the business actually needs.
How do I get your résumé or start a conversation?
The one-page résumé is a click away (the download button, or the “Résumé” shortcut if you've installed this as an app), and the fastest path is email or LinkedIn on the Contact section. For specifics like availability, work authorisation and notice period, just reach out — I'm happy to share directly.
